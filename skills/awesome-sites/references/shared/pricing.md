# What each app replaces, and what that costs

Prices checked 2026-11-13 by opening each product's pricing page, unless a row says "secondary source". USD per month unless noted. Plans change often; a row marked UNVERIFIED could not be read, so no number is given.

Use this file when recommending an app: say what it replaces and the paid price, and also say what the paid product has that the spec does not. Do not present a spec as a full replacement of a larger product. "Billed annually" prices are the monthly equivalent and cost more month to month.

**Cost of running the apps here.** Hosting on Sites is free through 2026-12-31. From 2027-01-01 a site with a database uses about 25 credits a month before traffic; a site without storage uses 0 (Plus allowance: 125 credits a month shared by all your sites, so roughly five database-backed sites). Traffic costs 16 credits per 100,000 requests, and one page view is usually 30 to 50 requests. Free API tiers cover the keys; see `api-keys.md`. A custom domain is about 25 credits a month plus the domain itself.

## Where a spec saves little or nothing

- **campaign-tracker, club-ledger, group-order-ledger, invoicing:** a free or near-free product already exists (콜라보매니저, 총무의가계부 at 3,600원, 장부AI, Zoho Invoice, Invoice Ninja free plan). Build these for control, privacy, or a feature the free product lacks, not to save money.
- **digital-storefront:** merchant-of-record platforms charge more per sale but handle VAT and sales tax for you. Selling worldwide through Stripe alone makes tax compliance your job.
- **feedback-analyzer, seo-audit, docs-chatbot, research-briefs:** the paid products are larger systems (data connectors, keyword databases, source indexes). The spec covers the small end of the use case.
